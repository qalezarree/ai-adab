What My Heart Knows

Some nights I look up at the moon and wonder if it remembers us the way I do. I think about the version of us that never argued, never doubted, never stayed quiet when we should have spoken. Maybe that life exists somewhere else. But I’m learning that love isn’t only about the life we imagine. It’s about the one we are brave enough to fight for, with honest words, open hands, and the courage to try again.

If this is our last try, I want it to be real. And if it isn’t enough, I want to know I gave my whole heart before I let go.
